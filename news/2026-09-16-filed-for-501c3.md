Today we filed the paperwork to make Saheeh AI a recognized 501(c)(3)
nonprofit, organized in Texas.

We have always run as a nonprofit in spirit: nobody here answers to
investors, and the only question behind a decision is whether it serves the
people we care for. Filing makes that a matter of record. It commits us, in
writing and under law, to a charitable purpose, and it puts the organization
under the oversight that comes with tax-exempt status.

## What it changes

- **Accountability.** A 501(c)(3) has to file public annual returns, keep
  its books open, and spend on its mission. We think that is exactly the
  standard a mental health project should be held to.
- **Support.** Once the IRS recognizes us, donations will be tax-deductible
  to the extent the law allows, and we will be able to apply for the grants
  and programs that are only open to recognized charities.
- **Nothing about the product.** The chat and the journal keep working as
  they do today. Accounts, data and the daily limits are unchanged.

## What happens next

The IRS reviews applications in its own time, often several months. Until it
decides, we cannot promise that a gift is deductible, so we are not asking
for donations yet. When the determination arrives, we will say so here.

We also published our [Terms of Use and Privacy Policy](/legal) today. They
spell out what we collect, why, and the limits of what an AI companion can
be. Read them; they are short on purpose.
